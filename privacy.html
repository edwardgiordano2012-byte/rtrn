Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

At Versa, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Versa, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name and email address when you sign up or make a purchase
  • Information you provide in the contract creation chat (party names, payment terms, etc.)
  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment provider — we never store your card details)
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and features used

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate and deliver your contracts
  • Process your payments
  • Send you your completed documents via email
  • Improve our product and fix bugs
  • Send occasional product updates (you can unsubscribe anytime)

3. We Never Sell Your Data

Versa does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

4. Data Security

We take security seriously. All data is transmitted using SSL encryption. Payment processing is handled by trusted third-party providers who are PCI-DSS compliant. We store only what we need to deliver your service.

5. Contract Data

The information you share to build your contracts is used only to generate your document. We do not share your contract details with any third parties.

6. Cookies

Versa uses minimal cookies to keep the site functioning properly and to understand basic usage patterns. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your data, ask us to delete your account and data, or opt out of any marketing communications at any time. Just email us at support@tryversa.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via email or a notice on our website.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@tryversa.com.
